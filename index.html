01 — The letter

Without excuses.

Hey...

I'm not making this because I expect a reply. I'm not making this because I want to convince you of anything.

I'm making this because sometimes a simple "sorry" doesn't feel like enough when you know you've hurt someone who mattered so much to you.

So I wanted to take the time to actually say everything I should have said.

No excuses. No "but". No trying to make myself look better.

Just me being honest with you.

02 — Before the apology

There was an us.

It all started with a message on Instagram.

5 June 2023.

One message somehow became conversations. Conversations became friendship. And somewhere along the way, you became one of the most important people in my life.

We became best friends.

There were stupid conversations, random moments, little memories that probably didn't look important at the time but somehow became some of the memories I remember the most.

And then came the moments that weren't just conversations anymore.

04 — What I got wrong

The part where I take responsibility.

My silence.

I stopped communicating when I should have talked things through.

My ego.

I let my ego become more important than fixing something that mattered.

Ignoring you.

You ignored me, and instead of breaking that cycle, I chose to ignore you too. That wasn't maturity. It was my mistake.

Not caring enough.

There were moments when I didn't show you the care, respect and importance you deserved.

05 — The apology

The apology, without the "but".

I'm sorry for the times I made you feel ignored.

I'm sorry for letting my ego speak louder than my feelings.

I'm sorry for not communicating when I should have.

I'm sorry for not respecting your feelings the way I should have.

I'm sorry that there were moments where I didn't care for you the way someone who truly valued you should have.

And I'm sorry that instead of fixing things, I sometimes chose silence.

I could give you a hundred reasons for why I behaved the way I did.

But none of them would change the fact that I hurt you.

So I'm not going to make excuses.

I was wrong.

06 — What I understand now

Some things become clear later.

I understand now that caring about someone isn't just about the good moments.

It's also about communicating when things become difficult.

It's about putting ego aside.

It's about listening instead of assuming.

And it's about respecting someone's feelings, even when you don't completely understand them.

I can't change what already happened.

But I can understand it. I can learn from it. And I can become better because of it.

07 — Thank you

For everything.

Thank you for being there.

Thank you for the conversations, the memories, the stupid jokes, the little moments and the big ones.

Thank you for loving me.

Thank you for becoming such an important part of my life.

Whatever happens from here, I don't want to pretend that none of it mattered.

It mattered to me.

One last thing.

I'm not asking you to forget what happened.

I'm not asking you to immediately forgive me.

I'm not even asking you to come back.

You don't owe me a response.

I just owed you an apology.

I hurt you, and whatever you choose after this, I'll respect it.

I don't know what happens after this. I just know I needed you to know that I'm sorry.
